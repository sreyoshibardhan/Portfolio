THE PROBLEM

Users were asked to type and upload information that could often be retrieved, some steps had no alternative when they failed, and many people dropped off before finishing.

MY SOLUTION

I redesigned the journey to fetch and verify what was already available, so users review and confirm instead of typing. Every automated step kept a manual alternative, so nobody got stuck.

Key decisions

Five decisions that shaped the journey, tagged with the phase they shipped in.

01Never leave users stuck waiting for a codePhase 1 + 2
ProblemIf a verification code didn’t arrive, users were blocked at the very first step.
What I didAdded an alternative way to receive the code that takes over automatically when the first one doesn’t arrive.
02Fetch first, ask lastPhase 1 + 2
ProblemUsers typed identity and address details that could often be retrieved, and were sometimes asked for the same thing twice.
What I didFetched what was available and showed it for confirmation, asking only for what was genuinely missing, with a manual route always available.
03Unblock the bank stepLive · Phase 1
ProblemSupport tickets showed bank linking was where most users gave up.
What I didGave users several ways to add a bank account, so one failed route never meant starting over.
04Create an early winLive · Phase 2
ProblemSignature came after the hardest steps, so progress felt slow.
What I didMoved it right after identity verification, a quick milestone that builds momentum.
05Ask less, explain morePhase 1 + 2
ProblemLong declaration forms and unclear or missing error messages made users stall.
What I didPrefilled known answers, asked one question at a time, and wrote plain-language errors with a clear next step.

What I learned

01Retrieve, don’t askEvery field a user doesn’t have to type is friction removed. Confirming retrieved data is faster and more accurate.
02Always keep a fallbackAutomation won’t work for everyone. Each automated step needs a manual alternative so no one gets stuck.
03Listen to support ticketsThe biggest blocker in bank verification came from support complaints, not the funnel dashboard.
