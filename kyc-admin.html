THE PROBLEM

Every application needed a manual review before it could be submitted to CDSL, NSE and BSE. When marketing campaigns brought in a surge of sign-ups, operations couldn’t keep up and new accounts waited longer to go live.

MY SOLUTION

Rule-based auto-approve and auto-reject checks now sort most applications automatically. Only the remaining cases reach a reviewer, and approved accounts go to the exchanges in one click, so activation keeps pace with volume spikes.
