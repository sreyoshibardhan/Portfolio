THE PROBLEM
  • A dated, desktop-first design that performed poorly on phones
  • High bounce rates on mobile
  • Complex data that was hard to read on small screens
  • Inconsistent visual design across pages
MY SOLUTION
  • Designed ~80% of pages in Figma using Google Stitch
  • Built working prototypes with Lovable
  • Mobile-optimised data visualisations
  • A consistent design system across the platform

Key design improvements

01Mobile-first approach
DetailDesigned from mobile up, so every screen size gets an optimal experience.
02Responsive data tables
DetailReimagined complex stock tables so they read easily on a phone.

What I learned

01AI speeds up designTools like Google Stitch and Lovable cut design time, leaving more room for research and iteration.
02Mobile-first is essentialStarting with mobile constraints leads to cleaner designs that scale up well.
03Let data decideAnalytics and testing validate design decisions and show their real impact.
